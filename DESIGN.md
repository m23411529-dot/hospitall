## Brand & Style
The design narrative balances clinical credibility with vibrant childhood optimism. Designed for a pediatric dialysis charity platform, it intentionally avoids grim, clinical sorrow in favor of human dignity, radiant resilience, and joyful empowerment. 

The aesthetic aligns with modern warm minimalism infused with joyful, friendly micro-details:
- **Emotional Stance:** Hopeful, transparent, empathetic, and urgent without resorting to pity. It focuses on life unlocked, dreams supported, and daily pediatric triumphs.
- **Visual Personality:** Soft rounded surfaces, buoyant illustrative framing, vibrant sunny energy, and spotless clarity. It combines trustworthy institutional rigor (for donors and medical sponsors) with warm, tender embrace (for families and young fighters).

## Colors
The palette conveys life, restoration, and sunny vitality:
- **Primary Teal (`#0D9488`):** Represents clinical renewal, purified water, healing, and balanced stability. Used for anchor navigation, primary confirmation states, and essential trust metrics.
- **Secondary Sunny Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Inspires warmth, morning light, cheerfulness, and childhood wonder. Applied to critical conversion triggers, donation progress bars, and celebration tags.
- **Tertiary Rose Coral (`#F43F5E`):** Represents the beating heart, blood filtration, vitality, and urgent care needs. Used for high-impact call-to-action badges, gift matching notices, and urgent critical care appeals.
- **Warm Sand Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7`):** Replaces cold hospital white with a soft, sunlit alabaster tone, removing sterile alienation while retaining high contrast and optical clarity. Deep warm slate (`#1C1917`) is used for text to guarantee readability in both Arabic and English script.

## Typography
`Readex Pro` is selected across all roles for its geometric balance and clear glyph rendering across both Arabic (RTL) and Latin (LTR) scripts. Its open counters and clean ascenders make long medical campaign reading comfortable while offering friendly, open character in headlines.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Semi-Bold and Bold weights with tight line heights to command attention and radiate celebratory energy.
- **Body & Storytelling:** Set with relaxed line heights (minimum 1.6x) to preserve fluid readability for both Arabic diacritics and English body copy.
- **Numbers & Data:** Arabic and Indic numerals must be crisp, elevated with semi-bold weights for currency goals, session counters, and impact milestones.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a responsive 12-column grid on desktop (conforming to a maximum container width of 1280px), transitioning to an 8-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile.

- **RTL Fluidity:** Structural alignment automatically flips for Arabic layout orientation. Spacing margins mirror precisely to protect visual balance.
- **Canvas Breathing Room:** Large vertical section gaps (`space-xl` and higher) prevent visual clutter, allowing high-contrast campaign cards and uplifting imagery to stand out without competing.
- **Information Density:** Compact spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) is strictly reserved for interior chip details, tags, and progress bar captions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through ambient, sun-tinted light and layered surfaces rather than sharp or muddy shadows:

- **Surface Layering:** The foundational background rests on warm sand (`#FDFBF7`). Interactive campaign containers and child profile cards use pure, luminous white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle ambient warmth.
- **Ambient Diffusion:** Shadows use a warm amber-slate tint (`rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)` to `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`) with broad blur radii (16px to 32px), creating a buoyant, floating effect.
- **Interactive Depth:** Floating donation bars and sticky action anchors employ a soft backdrop blur (12px frosted diffusion) to preserve context when scrolling through patient stories.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides a gentle, welcoming contour:
- **Base Components:** Buttons, inputs, and minor badges feature an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius, balancing structural legibility with softness.
- **Feature Cards & Modals:** Large campaign cards, donor receipt surfaces, and photo viewports use `rounded-xl` (24px / `1.5rem`) to feel friendly, accessible, and protective.
- **Badges & Progress Tracks:** Badges, chips, and linear meter tracks are pill-shaped (`9999px`) to echo drop-of-water, organic contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Donation CTA:** High-contrast amber background (`#F59E0B`) with crisp dark slate text (`#1C1917`) or punchy teal (`#0D9488`) with white text. Sized generously (minimum 48px height) with tactile pill or rounded corners, conveying urgency without alarm.
- **Secondary Action:** Ghost and tonal styles using a 1.5px border of Teal (`#0D9488`) or translucent Sand tinting.

### Progress Bars
- Dual-color animated tracks with a soft muted sand background track. The fill is rendered in warm amber or radiant teal, punctuated by an energetic coral target marker or celebratory spark icon at milestone completions.

### Campaign Cards
- White elevated surfaces featuring edge-to-edge photography of smiling children, framed by gentle top corner radii. The bottom half contains a clear dialysis session counter, amount funded, and a single prominent donation trigger.

### Badges & Chips
- Cheerful status tags (e.g., "Dialysis Session Funded", "Urgent Match 2x", "Kidney Hero") utilizing soft pastel fills paired with deeply saturated type (e.g., amber wash with dark amber text).

### Form Inputs & Quick-Donation Toggles
- Pre-filled donation amounts ($25, $50, $100 / Equivalent SAR or AED) presented as segmented selectable cards with clear active borders, large legible numbers, and concise impact labels (e.g., "Covers 1 Filter Kit").
